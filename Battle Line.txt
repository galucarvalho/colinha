Overwhelm the opponent in\nthe center, break through one of the flanks, or hold the position until the time has come\nfor a decisive move. How will you muster the battle line?\n\nPlayers:\nTwo\nPlaying Time: 30 minutes\nComponents:\n• This rulebook\n• 60 Troop cards, in six colors each with the values 1 to 10:  [10 = Elephants, 9 =\nChariots, 8 = Heavy Cavalry, 7 = Light Cavalry, 6 = Hypaspists, 5 = Phalangists,\n4 = Hoplites, 3 = Javalineers, 2 = Peltasts, 1 = Skirmishers]\n• Ten Tactics cards (white, ,vith different backs)\n• Nine Flags (plastic pieces)\n\nPREPARATION\nLayout the nine Flags in a line between the two players. Cut the Troop deck until one\nplayer has the higher number—that player shuffles and deals seven Troop cards to each\nplayer's hand. The first player to win three\nadjacent Flags (a Breakthrough) or any five Flags (an Envelopment) achieves victory.\nThe non-dealer plays first; play alternates thereafter. On his turn, a player selects either\none Troop card or one Tactics card from his hand and places it face up on his side of the\nbattle line. At the end of his turn, the player draws one card from either deck to refresh\nhis hand to seven. When both decks run out, no further cards can be drawn, but play\ncontinues.\n\nThe Flags are won by formations of Troop cards played adjacent to the Flags. The Tactics\ncards are used to influence the formations.\n\nTROOP CARDS\nA player selects one of his Troop cards and places it face up on his side of the line\nadjacent to one Flag. There are three imaginary card \"slots\" on each side of the flag. In\nthe course of the game each player may place a maximum of three Troop cards adjacent\nto each Flag, in order to create formations. The order in which the cards are played is\nirrelevant. To save space, later cards are placed partially covering former cards at the\nsame flag.\n\nWhen a player is unable to play any Troop card, he may pass or play a Tactics card on his\nturn, but his opponent still continues to play until the game is decided.     "text": "TACTICS CARDS\nThere are ten Tactics cards. Tactics cards are played instead of Troop cards to influence\nthe formations. A player may hold as many Tactics cards as he wishes within his hand\nsize of seven, but may never play more than one more than his opponent has played. That\nmeans, a player can always play a Tactics card unless he has already played more than\nhis opponent. Each Tactics card has a special function and belongs to one of the\nfollowing three categories.\n\n1. Morale Tactics\nThese cards are played into the player's layout in place of, and in a similar way to, Troop\ncards:\n\n• Leader (Alexander, Darius): The Leaders are wild cards. Play a Leader like any Troop\ncard, but define the color and value when the Flag is resolved. For example, you have a\nBlue 8 in your layout. If you add a Leader, it gives you the option of adding a Blue 6, 7, 9\nor 10 to make a Wedge, or an 8 to form a Phalanx. Each player may only have one\nLeader on his side of the battle line. If drawn, a second Leader remains unplayable in the\nplayer's hand until the end of the game.\n\n• Companion Cavalry: Play this card like any Troop card of value 8, but define its color\nwhen the Flag is resolved.\n\n• Shield Bearers: Play this card like any Troop card, but define its color and its value not\nlarger than 3 when the Flag is resolved.\n\n2. Environment Tactics\nThese cards are played face up on the player's near side of an unclaimed Flag, so that\nthere is enough space for the Troop cards to be placed next to the Flag.\n\nOnce played, the Tactics cards stay in their position until the end of the game.\n\n• Fog: The fog card disables all formations, and the Flag is merely decided on the basis\nof the total value of cards on each side.\n• Mud: The claim for this Flag is now based on four cards on either side, so the\nformations need to be expanded. This makes it particularly difficult to achieve a Wedge\nor Phalanx.\n\n3. Guile Tactics\nThese cards are played face up on the player's own side next to the Tactics card deck. All\ncards played there must be clearly visible and remain there until the end of the game.\n\n• Scout: The player draws a total of three cards from one or both decks. Then he chooses\nany two cards from his hand and places them face down on top of their respective deck or\ndecks."
