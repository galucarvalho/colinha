You may \nlook at your own Myth cards during the game but keep your Myth cards face-down \nand hidden from other players.\nEach Myth card has 2 constellations on it. The player with the \nmost points wins the game and is crowned Best \nAstronomer!\nIn case of a tie, the player with the most points from \nMyth Cards wins the game.
