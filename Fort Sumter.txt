Driven by Lincoln’s election\nand his platform that slavery cannot expand, the\nSecessionist player is exiting the US Constitu-\ntion to avoid having slavery legislated out of\nexistence. Both\nplayers secretly choose one of their objective cards\nto keep, then shuffle the other one (unrevealed)\nback into the objective card deck.\nStarting with the first player (Unionist player at\nthe beginning of Turn 1), players alternate taking\nturns playing one strategy card either for its value\nor its event. Play continues back and forth until\nboth players have played three strategy cards. The\nremaining strategy cards (one for each player) are\nset aside face down for the Final Crisis.\nPivotal space bonus actions are then performed by\nthe first player, followed by the opposing player.\nCrisis Dimensions are scored and then objec-\ntive cards are revealed and scored. Indicate each\nplayer’s current score by adjusting his Scoring\nCylinder on the Victory Point track (flipping to the\n“+10” side if needed). Check if conditions have\nbeen met for the Final Crisis and, if not, continue\nwith the next round.\nSequence of Actions Each Round\n•\t Deal four strategy cards to each player.\n•\t Deal two objective cards to each player.\n•\t Each player chooses one objective card to keep\nand then shuffles the other one back into the\ndeck.\n•\t Take turns playing strategy cards for their value\nor their events until each player has played three\ncards.\n•\t Set aside the remaining card for each player in\nhis Final Crisis space at the edge of the board.\n•\t Perform Pivotal Space bonus actions.\n•\t Score Crisis Dimensions.\n•\t Reveal and score objective cards.\n•\t Perform objective event if you scored your\nobjective.\n•\t Check for Final Crisis or end of third round.\nUp to three rounds of play are completed, then\nthe Final Crisis is conducted and final scoring\ndetermines the winner.\nPlaying Strategy Cards\nWhen played during a normal round, each strategy\ncard can be used for either its event or its value\nafter which it is removed from the game.
