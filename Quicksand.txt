    "text": "6\nHOW TO PLAY\nTHE PLAYER TURN\nDuring your turn, you must play 1 card from your\nhand, announce aloud which type of card you are\nplaying (color, shape, or wild card), then Activate\nall sand timers resting on tiles that match the\ncard you just played. For more information about\nActivating sand timers, see page 8.\nAfter you have finished applying the effects of the\ncard you played, draw a new card to replenish\nyour hand to its full size of 3. You must always have\n3 cards in your hand at the end of your turn.\nAfter this, the turn of the player to your left starts.\nThe game proceeds in this way until any victory\nor defeat conditions are triggered (see page 11).\nA game of Quicksand™ is played over a variable\nnumber of turns.\nOnce you begin the game, there’s no way to pause,\nso be sure everyone knows the rules and is ready to\nplay until the end.\nWhen you are ready to begin, starting from the\nmost advanced sand timer on the Gear track and\ngoing backwards, flip all of the sand timers so\nthat the sand starts flowing in all of them.\nThen, take a look at your cards and quickly discuss\nwith the other players to decide who will play first\n(remember, the sand is already flowing).\nAfter the starting player has been chosen, players\ntake turns playing clockwise.\nDuring the game, you can freely talk about the\ncards in your hand with the other players to work\nout a common strategy."
