    "text": "Short rules\nEach player receives 60 cards of the same color (silver or gold). Both players\nlay down the row cards in front of them on the table. Both players shuffle their\nown 58 number cards thoroughly (!) and put them into a draw pile. Each player\nthen picks up the first 6 number cards from his own respective draw pile, which\nmake up his hand. Draw to see who goes first.\ncThe game is played in turns. On your turn, you have to discard at least\ntwo (or more) of your cards from your hand (the two-card-minimum rule also\napplies when your own draw pile has run out!). Then, pick up cards from your\nown draw pile (how many depends, see below) to end your turn. The other\nplayer then begins his turn.\ncLay down your cards either exclusively on your own discard piles or ex­\nactly one card on one of the other player‘s discard piles and the rest on your\nown discard piles. You are never allowed to discard more than one card on your\nopponent‘s discard pile per round.\ncWhen discarding on your own discard piles the exact same rules for\ndiscarding from the original game apply (descending, ascending, backwards\ntrick). These rules do not apply when discarding a card onto your opponent‘s\ndiscard pile. Instead, the card discarded onto your opponent’s discard pile\n(either one) helps him improve his discard pile.\nExample: Tim wants to lay one of his cards on Sarah’s ascending pile. The 29\ncard is currently showing. Tim therefore has to lay a card that is lower (by\nany margin) than the 29. He lays down the 12 onto the pile, which improves\nSarah’s pile.\ncIf you lay down cards only on your pile, then you have to pick up two\ncards from your own draw pile (regardless of how many cards you discar­\nded). If you lay down a card on the opponent’s pile (and at least one card\non your own pile), then you have to pick up as many cards from your own\ndraw pile until you have 6 cards in your hand again.\nExample: Sarah discards 3 cards on her own piles. She then picks up 2 cards.\nTim lays 5 cards, 4 on his own piles and 1 on Sarah’s. Tim then picks up cards\nuntil he has 6 cards in his hand again.\ncWhoever discards all of his own 58 number cards wins the game! If at\nany time in the game you are unable to discard a minimum of two cards, you\nimmediately lose the game.\nComplete game rules\nEach player receives 60 cards of the\nsame color (silver or gold). Both play­\ners lay down the row cards (1–59 and\n60–2) in front of them on the table.\nThroughout the course of the game the\nnumber cards are laid to the right of\nthese cards.\nEach player shuffles his 58 number\ncards thoroughly (!) and puts them into\na face-down draw pile to the left of his\ntwo row cards.\nTip: To ensure that the cards are in fact\nshuffled well enough, each player can\nshuffle the cards of the other player.\nEach player then picks up the first 6\ncards from his own pile, which make up\nhis hand.\nRules for laying cards: Laying cards on your own piles\nDuring the game, each player repeatedly lays number cards on his card piles.\nWhen laying cards on your own discard piles the following rules for laying\ncards apply (they are exactly the same as the rules in the original “The Game”).\nNote: In each round, you can lay one card on your opponent’s discard piles.\nThese rules do not apply for cards laid on your opponent’s discard piles (see\n“Laying cards on your opponent’s piles”).\nWhen laying cards in your own ascending row of cards the card number of\neach card laid must always be greater than the card laid before it. The margin\nbetween the cards does not matter, e. g. 3, 11, 12, 13, 18, 20, 34, etc.\nImportant: To keep things from getting out of hand, lay the number cards\none on top of the other, rather than side by side. This will form one pile next\nto each row card with only the top card of each pile showing.\nSarah first lays the 4 on her ascending\npile, followed by the 8 and the 13.\nWhen laying cards in your own descending row of cards it’s exactly the oppo­\nsite: the card number of each card laid must always be less than the card laid\nbefore it, e. g. 54, 50, 48, 41, 40, 37, etc.\nIf you‘re already familiar with the original „The Game“, you only need to read\nthe short rules before playing. Here‘s how it works:\nOn your turn, you can lay a card on your own ascending pile that is exactly 10\nless than the number showing on the pile.\nExample: Normally, Tim would have to lay a\ncard on his own ascending pile that is greater\nthan 27. However, he has the 17 in his hand and\ncan play the card on his ascending pile, since\nit is exactly 10 less than the card showing.\nOn your own ascending pile, you can lay a card that is exactly 10 greater than\nthe number showing on the pile.\nExample: Normally, Sarah would have to lay\na card on her own descending pile that is less\nthan 35.
