    "text": "My Sudden But Inevitable Betrayal\n“SOMETIMES, THEY COME BACK.”\n\t\nI’ve heard that a lot. I didn’t think it’d be me, of course. In 2003, my last project at Wizards of the \nCoast was developing Bruce Glassco’s game “The House on the Hill.” I loved the game. It was my team’s \nsolution to a single player overlording a cooperative game. We made you care about your character, and \nfear letting someone else get all the good stuff. If you could be betrayed, you’d play cooperatively, but \nthink individually.\n\t\nIt was a great concept, and it got the best team. \nHasbro’s Rob Daviau crafted the draft version from \nBruce’s original manuscript. Then I put together a \nrock star crew—Bruce Cordell, Teeuwynn Woodruff, \nBill McQuillan, and many others—to make a rock star \ngame. We called it Betrayal at House on the Hill\n®.\n\t\nWith Betrayal in the books, I left to start my \ncompany, Lone Shark Games, right at the end of the \ndesign process. I figured that’d be the last I’d hear \nof the game.\n\t\nThen something happened. Right around the \nrelease of the 2nd Edition in 2010, people started \ntalking about Betrayal a ton. They’d come up \nand tell me that it was their favorite game, that \nit changed their lives. People like Cards Against \nHumanity co-creator Max Temkin, cartoonist \nPendleton Ward, and Doubleclicks singer Angela \nM. Webber. It influenced the work of fans like \nvideogame designer Zoë Quinn and Dead of Winter \ndesigner Jonathan Gilmour. I hired two of Betrayal’s \nbiggest fans, Elisa Teague and Liz Spain, to be game \ndesigners on my team.\n\t\nAnd at every convention, in every panel Q&A \nsession, I would get asked one question:\n\t\n“Would you ever consider doing more Betrayal?”\n\t\n“You should ask Wizards,” I’d say, and change \nthe subject. I understood where they came from. \nBetrayal is episodic, and everyone wants new \nepisodes of their favorite show. But actually making \nnew episodes was unlikely to happen. In a practical \nsense, I put the thought out of my mind. I hoped I’d \nget another shot at it, but the real world stands in \nthe way of such things. So, I kept designing new \ngames.\n\t\nWhile making the Apocrypha Adventure Card \nGame, I described it as “the spiritual successor to \nBetrayal.” These were bold words. As we made our \nmodern horror game shine, we revisited Betrayal to \nremind ourselves what made it tick. And we started \nthinking of all new ideas for it. Ideas which would \ngo nowhere unless . . .\n\t\n“Hey Mike, do you want to do more Betrayal?” \nThe voice on the phone was Mike Mearls, head of \nR&D for Dungeons & Dragons ®. Wizards wanted to \ncreate an expansion for Betrayal, and because of my \nhistory with the game, they thought of me first. It \nwas the day after my birthday. I assured Mike that \nyes, I’d like that very much.\n\t\nSo as we were developing one modern horror \ngame, we began—in secret and in the shadows—\nto develop a second. We kept it to ourselves, \ncodenaming it “Project Riboflavin” (aka “B2”). I \ninvited all those friends who told me they loved \nBetrayal to write haunts. Rob, Elisa, and Liz joined \nme on the core team. Some of their stuff was mind-\nbogglingly strange. All of it was great.\n\t\nWe added cards, tokens, and rooms that led you \nall around the inner spaces of the house. And we \nput a roof on the house, because every house needs \na roof.\n\t\nSo yeah, when someone says “sometimes they \ncome back,” you should listen.\n\t\n\t\nThey always come back.\nMike Selinker\nLead Developer, Betrayal at House on the Hill \nCREDITS\nDesign: Mike Selinker with Elisa Teague and Liz Spain\nDevelopment: Chad Brown, Chris Dupuis, Gaby Weidling\nAdditional Development: Thomas Ball, Rob Daviau, Bryan \nHawley, Paul Peterson, Ben Petrisor, Javier Quintero, Keith \nRichmond, Aviva Schecterson, Lucy Tibbits\nHaunts: Peter Adkison, Christopher Badell, Keith Baker, \nThomas Ball, John Borba, Chad Brown, Bart Carroll, Quelle \nChris, Andy Collins, Rob Daviau, Michael Dunlap, Chris Dupuis, \nDon Eubanks, Justin Gary, Jonathan Gilmour, Bruce Glassco, \nEli Halpern, Will Hindmarch, Jerry Holkins, Mons Johnson, \nGwendolyn Kestrel, Richard Malena, Mikey Neumann, \nPaul Peterson, Ben Petrisor, Marie Poole, Zoë Quinn, Javier \nQuintero, Keith Richmond, Mike Robles, Tifa Robles, Anita \nSarkeesian, Aviva Schecterson, F.
